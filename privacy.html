Privacy Policy

DueEasy. Last updated: October 14, 2026.

What this tool is

DueEasy is a free information tool that runs entirely in your web browser. It helps you check a medical bill, screen for hospital financial assistance, and draft an insurance appeal letter.

What we collect

Nothing.

  • There is no account, no sign up, and no server that receives your information.
  • Everything you type stays on your device, in your browser.
  • If you tap "Save on this device," your entries are stored only in your browser's local storage on that device. No one else can see them.
  • If you tap "Erase everything," the saved copy is deleted.
  • We do not sell data. We do not run advertising trackers. We do not use analytics that receive anything you type.

What to be careful about

  • Anyone who can unlock your phone or computer and open this page may see entries you saved on the device. Use Erase everything on shared devices.
  • Letters you copy, download, print, or send are handled by the apps and services you choose to use for that. Their privacy rules apply once the letter leaves this page.

Not advice

This tool gives general information and document help. It is not legal advice, medical advice, or a guarantee that a bill, denial, or assistance decision will change. Your plan documents and denial letter control your rights and deadlines.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, payments, or any data collection, this policy will be updated and dated before that version launches, and the change will be stated plainly on the home page.

Feedback form

The home page links to an optional feedback form run by Google Forms. That form is separate from this tool. It does not collect email addresses, and it asks only which part of the tool you used and whether it helped. Never put personal medical or billing details in it: no names, claim numbers, member IDs, dates of service, diagnoses, or copies of bills or letters. Anything you choose to type into that form goes to Google and to us as form responses, so keep it general.
